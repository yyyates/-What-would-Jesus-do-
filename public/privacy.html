Privacy

Last updated: November 22, 2026. Questions? Contact yatesbellamy@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you ask a question, we send the text of your question to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate an answer. We do not ask for your name or email, and we do not save your questions or answers on our servers. Our server keeps your IP address in memory for one day only to enforce the daily free-question limit.

Check-in reminders

Reminders are off unless you turn them on. If you choose notifications, we save the address your browser gives us for sending them, plus the time and time zone you picked. If you choose email, we save your email address, time and time zone, and send emails through our email provider, Resend. Subscriber details are stored with Upstash. We use this only to send the reminders you asked for and never sell or share it. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can turn notifications off on the reminders page or in your browser; when you stop, we delete your details.

Our AI provider

Questions are processed by Anthropic under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to train its models on your questions. See anthropic.com/legal for details.

Payments

Premium payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. Stripe shares your email and subscription status with us so we can provide Premium.

Cookies and tracking

If you subscribe, we set one cookie that keeps you signed in to Premium on that device. Premium members' past questions are saved only in their own browser, not on our servers. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Please don't share sensitive details

You can ask about anything, but avoid including full names, addresses, or other details that identify you or others.

Children

This service is not directed to children under 13.
